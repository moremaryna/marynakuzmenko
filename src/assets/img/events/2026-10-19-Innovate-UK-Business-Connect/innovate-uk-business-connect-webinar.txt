Over 350 people joined us yesterday for the webinar organised by Innovate UK Business Connect. 
I’m sure there are easily ten times more people who are genuinely interested in starting a business in the UK and getting the official business support from the UK Government. 

At the webinar, I spoke a bit about how I’ve been supported since 2017 with Petiole Pro. And now, literally this Friday, I have a meeting with my knowledge transfer partner to talk about intellectual property for MooMore and some other aspects of the founder’s journey 🐮 

The main point: whether you’re a startup or big enough to think about scaling your business, there’s a suite of support that fits your needs. 

To name a few - here are the 5 ways Innovate UK Business Connect can support you 👇 

1️⃣ Sector newsletter: free funding calls, insights and events in your inbox (the team really works hard to collect everything relevant - I read every issue 🙏)

2️⃣ Webinars: free sessions to learn and connect with people in your niche. Next webinar in the series "Understanding the Innovation Journey" is on 8 December about mentoring. 

3️⃣ Events: brokerage events, workshops and the annual AgriFood Innovation Showcase (I've attended most of them!)

4️⃣ Sector experts: as I mentioned, a company has Knowledge Transfer Manager who helps to refine your idea and makes warm introductions 

5️⃣ Support as you grow: help is available on each stage. Either it investor readiness, IP rights or search of university partnerships for consortium - explain your innovation and ask.
